Selected research projects

My share of awarded research funding totals €1,038,847.

Rough paths for financial and demographic data

Universiti Malaya Research Excellence Grant · Co-investigator

Led by Dharini Pathmanathan, this collaboration combines functional data analysis, spatial statistics and rough path theory. It studies how signatures of observed trajectories can help characterise financial volatility and population change, and how they can support dimension reduction in high-dimensional data.

Universiti Malaya project listing

Time-series analysis of historical maps

Federal Agency for Cartography and Geodesy (BKG) · Collaborative project · Started 2022

Part of the Gauss Centre’s research on changes in geospatial data. The work combines map classification with statistical modelling to reconstruct changes in settlements, agricultural land, forests and coastlines from historical maps.

Leibniz University Hannover project record

AgrImOnIA · Agricultural impact on Italian air

Fondazione Cariplo · Co-investigator · 2021–2023

The teams in Bergamo, Turin, Milan-Bicocca and Hannover studied how livestock activity and meteorology relate to particulate matter in Lombardy. The project produced an open dataset combining monitoring, weather, emissions and agricultural records, and a comparison of models for predicting PM₂.₅ at unobserved stations.

University of Turin project record · Dataset and data descriptor · PM₂.₅ prediction study

Estimating high-dimensional spatial dependence

German Research Foundation (DFG) · Project lead · Listed in 2019

The project studies how to estimate spatial interactions from observations when the dependence structure is unknown. It uses statistical learning to infer connections beyond those suggested by simple geographical proximity, with applications to environmental monitoring.

Leibniz University Hannover project record · Related research on learning spatial weights

Spatial and spatiotemporal GARCH models

German Research Foundation (DFG) · Principal investigator · 2019–2023

This project developed models for volatility that depends on neighbouring locations and evolves over time. The work separates spatial dependence in the level of a process from spatial dependence in the size of its deviations. Applications include modelling local uncertainty in property prices and volatility in financial returns.

Leibniz University Hannover project record · Spatial GARCH framework · Financial returns application

Detection and surveillance of spatial clusters

Viadrina Center B/ORDERS IN MOTION · Research group lead · 2017–2018

The group developed methods for finding clusters and monitoring how spatial dependence changes over time. Outputs include work on spatial ARCH and on how wind direction changes the spatial transmission of particulate matter concentrations.

European University Viadrina project record · Spatial ARCH paper · Wind direction and particulate matter

Research interests

I am interested in statistical data science and statistical methodology for data in multidimensional spaces, e.g., geo-referenced data. Most of my papers have one theory in common - Tobler's first law of geography: "everything is related to everything else, but near things are more related than distant things."
