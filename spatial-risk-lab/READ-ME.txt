Spatial Risk Lab — privacy and iOS support pages
Prepared 27 October 2026. Contact: spatialrisklab@gmail.com. Publisher: Philipp Otto.

Files to host together in the same public website directory:
  privacy-ios.html       iOS/iPadOS privacy policy
  support-ios.html       iOS/iPadOS support page
  privacy-android.html   Android privacy policy with the new contact address

The pages are self-contained English HTML with responsive light/dark styling.
No external fonts, images, scripts, analytics, forms or tracking are included.
Internal links are relative, so the pages can share any public hosting directory.

App Store Connect:
  Privacy Policy URL: the public HTTPS address of privacy-ios.html
  Support URL: the public HTTPS address of support-ios.html
Google Play Console:
  Privacy policy: the public HTTPS address of privacy-android.html

These are prepared files, not published pages. No deployment or store submission
was performed. After hosting, check each address without signing in.

Source review: iOS v40 and Android v5. The iOS policy distinguishes Apple Maps
network requests from gameplay held on-device. It also covers voluntary sharing,
website hosting requests, OS backups and correspondence sent to the Gmail address.
The prepared Android hosting file in Android v5 has the same updated contents.

No native app source or release binary was changed by this task. Existing builds
still contain the previous contact email and should use the new address in the
next app update. The previous multilingual iOS page is preserved as an archive;
use privacy-ios.html from this package for the new English website policy.

Validation: HTML structure, mail links and relative page links were checked
locally. Browser rendering could not be checked in this environment because
local-page previews are blocked. Review the layout after hosting.

Requirements checked against:

https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information
