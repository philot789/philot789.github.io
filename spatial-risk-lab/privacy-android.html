Spatial Risk Lab: Android privacy policy

Publisher: Philipp Otto. Contact: ph.otto789@icloud.com. Updated 15 October 2026.

Data on your device

Spatial Risk Lab stores your scores, achievements, reading progress, unfinished mission and settings locally on your device. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, research uploads or automatic online synchronization. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone. All simulations and map data are bundled or generated locally.

Sharing and external links

You choose whether to share a result image or challenge link. A challenge includes the world, simulation seed, model, constraints and optionally your score. The app sends this content to the service you choose in Android's share menu. That service applies its own privacy policy. Following a research or website link opens your browser; the visited website may process information such as IP addresses under its own policy.

Your choices

You can reset scores, achievements and reading progress in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local files under Android's normal storage rules. The app disables Android cloud backup. It does not collect data from children or any other players for research. Any future research feature would require a separate explanation and appropriate consent before data collection.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Philipp Otto at the email address above. This policy describes the current Android version. It will be updated if the app's data practices change.
